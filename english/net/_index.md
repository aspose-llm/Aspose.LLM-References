{{% alert color="warning" %}}
**No built-in LLM is included in Aspose.LLM**, so customers need to choose and install their favorite LLM on their own. A preset is configuration only: the model file is obtained separately on your machine, and its use is governed by the model publisher's license, not the Aspose license. Verifying and complying with that license is the responsibility of the user, not of Aspose Pty Ltd. See the [Supported LLMs](https://docs.aspose.com/llm/net/product-overview/supported-llms/) page for the per-family license overview, and the [AI Governance and Risk Management](https://trust.aspose.com/app-security/ai-governance-and-risk-management/) policy at the Aspose Trust Center: the product integrates AI models, it does not include or provide them.
{{% /alert %}}
